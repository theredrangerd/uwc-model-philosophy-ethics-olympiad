## 1. Executive Vision & Philosophy

### 1.1 Shift in Purpose: From Recruitment Hook to Academic Depth & Sneak Peek
The main landing page (`index.html`) is engineered for rapid recruitment at LifeCon: high visual impact, fast scanning, tournament prestige, and a clear call-to-action to register on CIMS. 

**The Resources Tab / Socratic Archive serves a complementary, immersive purpose:**
- It provides a **sneak peek into what a day at MPEO is like** for the **curious 10–20%** of students who want to look under the hood.
- It moves beyond marketing slogans to provide **real academic substance**: authentic past tournament case packs, classroom lecture decks, philosophical framework toolkits, and scoring rubrics.
- The aesthetic feels like entering a private, illuminated scholarly archive—intellectually serious, visually captivating, distraction-free, and meticulously organized.

## 4. Socratic Frameworks & Scoring Rubric

### 4.1 Philosophical Framework Matrix
Six core moral traditions tested in tournament match rounds:
1. **Utilitarianism (Consequentialism)**: Maximizing aggregate utility (Bentham, Mill, Singer).
2. **Kantian Deontology (Duty Ethics)**: Categorical Imperative; treating humanity as ends in themselves (Kant, Korsgaard).
3. **Virtue Ethics (Character & Eudaimonia)**: Golden mean and practical wisdom (Aristotle, MacIntyre, Nussbaum).
4. **Ethics of Care (Relational Theory)**: Empathy, mutual vulnerability, and repairing relational harm (Gilligan, Held, Noddings).
5. **Rawlsian Justice (Social Contract)**: Veil of ignorance and difference principle (Rawls, Scanlon).
6. **Principlism (Applied Bioethics)**: The 4 clinical pillars—Autonomy, Beneficence, Non-Maleficence, Distributive Justice (Beauchamp & Childress).

### 4.2 Official Judging Criteria (5 Dimensions — Scored Out of 10 Pts Each)
1. **Clarity & Presentation** (*Out of 10 Pts*): Systematic, structured presentation directly addressing prompt.
2. **Moral Dimensions** (*Out of 10 Pts*): Precise identification of underlying ethical principles.
3. **Counter-Perspectives** (*Out of 10 Pts*): Nuanced acknowledgement of trade-offs and opposing arguments.
4. **Collaborative Dialogue** (*Out of 10 Pts*): Respectful, constructive Socratic discourse in commentary steps.
5. **Response to Judges' Q&A** (*Out of 10 Pts*): Poise, logical depth, and agility with edge-case hypotheticals.
