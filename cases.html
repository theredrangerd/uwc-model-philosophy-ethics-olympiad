COMPETITIVE METHODOLOGY

How MPEO Deliberates Any Case

The 5-step analytical framework taught in our weekly workshops to systematically deconstruct moral dilemmas.

STEP 01
🎯

Isolate Core Friction

Identify the genuine moral disagreement beneath emotional rhetoric. Distinguish empirical uncertainties from fundamental value conflicts (e.g. Autonomy vs. Beneficence).

STEP 02
👥

Map All Stakeholders

Chart the direct and indirect impacts on all affected groups, paying special attention to disenfranchised voices, non-human entities, and future generations.

STEP 03
⚖️

Apply Ethical Lenses

Evaluate the dilemma across major philosophical traditions: Utilitarian utility, Kantian categorical duties, Aristotelian virtue, Gilligan's Care Ethics, and Rawlsian justice.

STEP 04
🔍

Acknowledge Trade-offs

Intellectual honesty is paramount. No legitimate dilemma has a cost-free solution. Explicitly acknowledge the moral sacrifices required by your chosen stance.

STEP 05
🤝

Collaborative Synthesis

Listen actively to the opposing team's perspective. Synthesize their constructive critique into a refined, robust policy recommendation that judges reward.

CASE 01 ✦ Senior Olympiad 2025 🤖 AI & Biomedical Ethics

The Autonomous Triage Protocol

“

During a regional health emergency, a metropolitan hospital’s intensive care unit is inundated with critical respiratory failure patients. Facing a strict deficit of ECMO and mechanical ventilator units, the clinical ethics board deploys 'Aegis-Triage'—a validated predictive machine learning system designed to allocate life support.

✦ The Ethical Dilemma

The algorithm maximizes cumulative Quality-Adjusted Life Years (QALYs) by deprioritizing individuals with severe chronic comorbidities and elderly patients whose statistical survival probability is lower. However, community advocates note that historical socioeconomic disparities have resulted in higher baseline comorbidity rates among marginalized populations, meaning the algorithm systematically denies care to vulnerable demographic groups.

✦ Guiding Deliberation Questions for Teams

  • Is a purely utilitarian allocation (maximizing QALYs saved) morally justifiable when it compounds historical systemic injustice?
  • Should a lottery system or 'first-come, first-served' model be favored over algorithmic scoring despite saving fewer total lives?
  • How can medical practitioners balance the duty of individual beneficence with collective public health triage?
